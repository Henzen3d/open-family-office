## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-refined, confidential wealth intelligence platform engineered for multi-generational family offices and strategic stewards. The visual language balances sober institutional stewardship with inviting, high-craft modernity. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep charcoal and navy-slate foundations that emulate dark executive suites, eliminating stark black in favor of rich, optical depth.
- **Luminous Radiance:** Focal metrics, yield trajectories, and vital portfolio milestones radiate in warm amber, supported by deliberate, micro-dosed accents of periwinkle and frosted lilac.
- **Generous Soft Geometry:** Friendly yet rigorously controlled forms utilizing expansive radii, layered pill controls, and tactile, velvety container surfaces.
- **Subtle Luminescence:** Micro-glows and frosted inner borders define spatial hierarchy, avoiding harsh drop shadows or unrefined flat containers.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated nocturnal tiers:

- **Primary (`#E0A96D`):** Warm Amber. Anchors core financial intelligence, active navigation pills, aggregate values, and primary actionable states. Provides high warmth against nocturnal backdrops without visual aggression.
- **Secondary (`#8B9DF0`):** Soft Periwinkle. Used for liquidity instruments, asset allocation delta, comparative benchmark lines, and secondary structural accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C9A4D8`):** Frosted Lilac. Applied to alternative investments, trust and philanthropic allocations, and nuanced data visualization accents.
- **Neutral Core (`#0D111A` to `#222B3D`):**
  - **Canvas Base:** `#0D111A` — absolute backdrop providing limitless contrast.
  - **Surface Level 1:** `#141A26` — structured view containers, pinned sidebars, and card groups.
  - **Surface Level 2:** `#1C2333` — primary analytical cards and module blocks.
  - **Surface Level 3 / Active Surface:** `#222B3D` — hovered interactive cards, nested drawers, and floating panels.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle translucent overlays (`rgba(224, 169, 109, 0.12)` for highlighted elements, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` for structural separation).
- **Text & Feedback Hierarchy:**
  - High Contrast Pure Neutral: `#F5F7FA` (95% opacity for headline reading comfort).
  - Muted Label Slate: `#94A3B8`.
  - Positive Performance: `#4ADE80` (Muted Mint).
  - Exposure/Drawdown: `#F87171` (Crimson Rose).

## Typography

The type scale integrates the balanced, modern geometric architecture of **Manrope** for primary asset aggregates, portfolio balances, and view heads with the neutral, hyper-readable clarity of **Hanken Grotesk** across extensive accounting data, audit trails, and multi-currency ledgers.

Rules for application:
- Numeric totals and balance headers must render with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` to guarantee vertical baseline tracking in transactional reports.
- Capitalization in `label-sm` applies a subtle upper track (`letterSpacing: 0.04em`) when designating asset classes or risk bands.
- Paragraph copy never drops below 13px (`body-sm`) to preserve rigorous legibility across desktop viewports and tablet control sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model relies on a controlled 12-column responsive grid framed by deliberate interior breathing room to project composure and order.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid, `margin: 2.5rem` (40px), `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Sidebar docked at fixed 280px width; dashboard modules dynamically occupy 3, 4, 6, 8, or 12 spans.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 1.5rem` (24px), `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px). Collapsible utility bar converts to floating dock.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column flow, `margin-mobile: 1rem` (16px), `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (16px). Multi-column metric groups collapse into horizontal-swipe pill stacks.

### Rhythm & Density
- Micro-elements (icon-to-text, status-indicator pairing): `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
- Internal card padding: `space-lg` (24px) for desktop, reducing to `space-md` (16px) on compact mobile viewports.
- Section-to-section flow: `space-xl` (40px) to sustain deliberate isolation between divergent wealth buckets (e.g., Public Equities vs. Venture Syndicates).

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial elevation relies on **tonal containment, frosted inner edge illumination, and ambient low-frequency glows** rather than opaque drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Stacking
1. **Canvas (`#0D111A`):** Deep ground plane. Houses micro radial backdrop blurs (`rgba(224, 169, 109, 0.03)` blur: 120px) under main metric clusters to create ambient illumination.
2. **Sub-Containers (`#141A26`):** Outer panels and widget groups. Borders are built using `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Modules (`#1C2333`):** Primary analytical cards. Raised with:
   - Outer shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
   - Inner highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
4. **Interactive Highlights / Overlays (`#222B3D`):** Dropdowns, modals, and hovered active cards. Enhanced with warm luminescence:
   - Glow signature: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(224, 169, 109, 0.12)`.
   - Border signature: `1px solid rgba(224, 169, 109, 0.28)`.

### Frosted Translucency
Overlays, dialog backs, and sticky navigation headers leverage dynamic backdrop filtering (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`) with background tint `rgba(20, 26, 38, 0.75)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates generous curvature (`level 3`), combining structural stability with modern soft ergonomics:

- **Pill Primitives (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Applied to interactive buttons, filter switches, status badges, contextual tagging, and floating command triggers.
- **Card Shells (`rounded-xl` / 48px on main dashboards, 32px for inner modules):** Substantial corner radii provide architectural softness without sacrificing spatial efficiency.
- **Input Fields & Selectors (`16px` to `24px`):** Nested forms match the internal curve of their parent modules.
- **Nested Radii Hierarchy:** When containers sit inside larger cards, the inner component radius scales downward uniformly ($R_{inner} = R_{outer} - Padding$) to prevent visual tension.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill shape. Background is vibrant Warm Amber (`#E0A96D`) with text `#0D111A` in `label-lg` (`fontWeight: 600`). On hover, introduces an ambient amber glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(224, 169, 109, 0.4)`) and slight upward scale.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#1C2333` with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F5F7FA`. On hover, surface shifts to `#222B3D` with edge color shifting to periwinkle `rgba(139, 157, 240, 0.4)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#94A3B8`. Hover states introduce subtle `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill and crisp text transition to `#F5F7FA`.

### Interactive Chips & Status Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill geometry with light-absorbing dark fills (`rgba(x, y, z, 0.15)`) coupled with radiant micro-pips:
  - Performing / Liquid: Tinted `#4ADE80` with a 6px breathing green indicator.
  - Review Needed: Tinted `#E0A96D` with a warm amber pip.
  - Capital Call / Action: Tinted `#C9A4D8` with lilac badge tone.
- **Filter Switchers:** Contained in a `#141A26` pill track. The active item glides smoothly across slots via a `#1C2333` raised pill with `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.

### Cards & Analytical Widgets
- **Multi-layered Cards:** Base layer in `#1C2333` framed with a hairline border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Internal metric pods within the card adopt `#141A26` with `rounded-lg` (24px) for subtle contrast inversion.
- **Accented Data Displays:** Vital metric highlights incorporate a radial backdrop wash in amber or periwinkle that bleeds softly from the top-right corner of the card.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Text Inputs:** Pill-shaped height (44px) or rounded-lg (16px). Fill `#141A26`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- **Focused State:** Border brightens to `#E0A96D` paired with an immediate subtle outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(224, 169, 109, 0.15)`.

### Lists & Ledger Tables
- **Rows:** Borderless rows set on alternating low-frequency zebra tints (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.01)`) or separated by hair-thin gradients fading at cell margins.
- **Hover:** Entire row highlights to `#222B3D` with a smooth 150ms ease, rounding dynamically at left and right extremities.

### Checkboxes & Segment Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 20px rounded squares (`rounded: 6px`) in `#141A26` with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`. Active state transitions to `#E0A96D` background with dark `#0D111A` vector checkmark.
