## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an executive, optimistic, and deeply trustworthy financial operating environment tailored for high-net-worth family offices. Rooted in contemporary wealth stewardship, the aesthetic fuses crisp geometric clarity with soft, ambient warmth—evoking quiet confidence, meticulous order, and discretion.

The design movement combines **Corporate Modernism** with subtle **Tactile & Soft Luminescent Finishes**:
- **Canvas Atmosphere:** Clean, low-fatigue porcelain backdrops (`#F5F7FB`) that let white elevated modules float naturally without visual noise.
- **Sculpted Card Surfaces:** Ultra-crisp `#FFFFFF` container tiles paired with whisper-thin borders and ethereal blue-tinted ambient glows.
- **Chromatic Warmth & Energy:** A refined royal periwinkle primary tone balanced by pastel accent gradients (apricot, lilac, and azure) that distinguish multi-currency vaults and complex family holdings without feeling juvenile.
- **Executive Typography:** Confident modern geometric headers coupled with razor-sharp tabular numbers for dense, accurate balance sheets and movement records.

## Colors

The palette balances administrative gravitas with accessible, friendly refinement:

- **Primary (`#3E52D5`):** The signature royal periwinkle/indigo hue. Anchors high-priority actions ("Send", "Move Money", active navigation states) with a luminous, soft glow.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber gold. Deployed across secondary metrics, annual comparison vectors, and multi-asset indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6`):** Soft violet orchid. Reserved for multi-tier sub-accounts, liquidity transfers, and custom multi-currency card skins.
- **Neutral Palette:** 
  - Primary text: Ink Slate (`#0F172A`) ensures AAA contrast against porcelain canvases.
  - Secondary text: Subdued Slate (`#64748B`) handles data captions, dates, and account hashes.
  - Borders: Feather Gray (`#E2E8F0` at 60% opacity) provides ghost structure.
  - Canvas: Porcelain Fog (`#F5F7FB`), transitioning to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) on card containers.
- **Accent Pastels (Multi-Currency Layers):**
  - Euro/BRL Vaults: Soft Cornflower Gradient (`#60A5FA` → `#3E52D5`).
  - Liquidity/Yield: Warm Apricot Gradient (`#FBBF24` → `#F97316`).
  - Venture/Trust: Muted Lilac Gradient (`#C084FC` → `#A855F7`).

## Typography

Typography pairs **Manrope** for confident display headers and critical wealth metrics with **Hanken Grotesk** for hyper-legible body reading, ledger tables, and metadata tags.

- **Tabular Numerics:** All currency figures, percentages, dates, and transaction columns must activate `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` and `"cv05" 1` to ensure strict vertical columnar alignment.
- **Metric Scaling:** Quantitative numbers (`metric-xl`) adopt bold weights with negative tracking to impart substance without dominating card footprints.
- **Label Discipline:** Micro-labels and filter badges employ uppercase or medium-weight `label-sm` to maintain instant optical recognition across dense desktop views.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a fluid 12-column modular grid structured around a persistent vertical command rail (left navigation) and an expansive multi-card workspace:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Fixed 80px slim navigation rail expanding to 240px; fluid 12-column content container with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. High-priority cards (Wallet Balances, Spend Limits) stack above data tables.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-column flow with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 0.75rem`. Action bars collapse into horizontal pill swipers.
- **Card Padding Cadence:** Internal card padding adheres strictly to `space-lg` (24px) for prominent dashboards, reducing to `space-md` (16px) for compact listing modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows in favor of ambient multi-stop blurs tinted with indigo and neutral charcoal:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat porcelain plane (`#F5F7FB`).
- **Level 1 (Module Tile):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle double shadow:
  - `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(62, 82, 213, 0.05)`
  - Outlined with `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Pills & Popovers):** Elevated primary action triggers ("Send", active quick-contact avatars):
  - `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(62, 82, 213, 0.35)`
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Drawer Overlays):** Deep ambient spread:
  - `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(62, 82, 213, 0.06)`
- **Glassmorphic Floating Accents:** Used sparingly on chart tooltips and segmented filter chips using `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` top-edge bevel.

## Shapes

The design system embraces refined rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Cards & Data Modules:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) provides a friendly yet architectural canvas framing.
- **Action Buttons & Chips:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for quick transfers, modal triggers, and status badges, echoing the reference navigation controls.
- **Interactive Inputs:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for search inputs and currency text fields.
- **Nested Card Pockets (Stacked Multi-Currency Wallets):** Custom organic cut-outs and sculpted scalloped lips (`rounded-xl` top edges with 16px corner sweeps) to replicate the layered physical leather wallet metaphor seen in executive portfolio views.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Send / Move Money):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#3E52D5` fill, white typography (`headline-sm`), with `space-sm` vertical and `space-xl` horizontal padding. Accompanied by a signature ambient indigo halo shadow (`rgba(62, 82, 213, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Pill Button:** White background (`#FFFFFF`), Slate ink (`#0F172A`), fine ghost border (`#E2E8F0`), with soft ambient shadow on hover.
- **Circular Icon Button:** Pill-symmetric 44x44px round targets for standalone tools (`+` add beneficiary, calendar chevron triggers).

### Action Bar & Quick Action Chips
- Horizontally distributed pill buttons aligned in the top workspace bar. Selected state takes primary indigo fill; resting states take crisp white fill with faint Slate icons.

### Multi-Currency Stacked Card
- Layered wallet component showcasing multi-asset balances (EUR, USD, BRL). Each layer features soft distinct pastels:
  - Top card: Soft Cerulean (`#93C5FD` to `#3E52D5`).
  - Middle card: Marigold Amber (`#FCD34D` to `#F59E0B`).
  - Base pocket: Muted Orchid (`#D8B4FE` to `#8B5CF6`).
- Integrated pill counter badge (`4 cards`) and an overlaid circular pill action trigger (`+`).

### Quick Transfer Contact Carousel
- Circular avatars (48px) framed in subtle neutral borders. Active or selected contact features an indigo outline ring (`ring-2 ring-[#3E52D5] ring-offset-2`). Accompanied by a leading `+` add pill and trailing next button.

### Tables & Transaction Lists
- Borderless rows separated by `0.5rem` transparent gutters or whisper-thin dividers (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.5)`).
- Typography layout: Entity name (`body-md` bold), transaction timestamp (`body-sm` muted slate), tabular currency value (`metric-md`), and masked settlement account in mono-spaced tabular styling.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), porcelain background (`#FFFFFF` or `#EEF2F6`), subtle search icon prefix, and native keyboard shortcut tag (`⌘K`) in muted slate.
