# Crisis & Contingency Playbook — OpenFamilyOffice

> **Operational Triggers, Safety Thresholds, and Automated Action Plans for Adverse Economic Scenarios.**

---

## 1. Philosophy: Preparedness Over Prediction

Economic cycles, client concentration risk, inflation spikes, and macroeconomic shocks cannot be predicted with precision. However, a family and small business can build an antifragile financial posture by establishing **hard quantitative triggers** linked to **pre-committed action playbooks**.

Instead of discussing what to do during a crisis, OpenFamilyOffice defines automated alerts and step-by-step checklists that activate instantly when specific thresholds are breached.

---

## 2. Quantitative Trigger Matrix

| Risk Domain | Metric Monitored | Safe Green Zone | Warning Amber Zone | Critical Red Zone | Activated Playbook |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Household Runway** | Months of essential living expenses in liquid reserves | $\ge 6.0\text{ months}$ | $3.0\text{ to }5.9\text{ months}$ | $< 3.0\text{ months}$ | **Playbook A: Household Cash Compression** |
| **Fix Serviços Runway** | Months of fixed operating costs in working capital | $\ge 3.0\text{ months}$ | $1.5\text{ to }2.9\text{ months}$ | $< 1.5\text{ months}$ | **Playbook B: Business Cash Preservation** |
| **Client Concentration** | Share of gross revenue from a single client | $< 25\%$ | $25\%\text{ to }40\%$ | $> 40\%$ | **Playbook C: Client Concentration Defense** |
| **Inadimplência (Delinquency)**| % of Accounts Receivable overdue $> 30\text{ days}$ | $< 5\%$ | $5\%\text{ to }15\%$ | $> 15\%$ | **Playbook D: Receivables Recovery** |
| **Validation Venture (Facilita)**| Cumulative cash burn without validating unit economics | Within budgeted cap | $> 80\%$ of test budget | $100\%$ of budget without break-even | **Playbook E: Venture Circuit Breaker** |
| **MEI Legal Revenue Cap** | YTD Gross Revenue vs LC 238 limit | $< 75\%$ of annual cap | $75\%\text{ to }95\%$ of annual cap | $> 95\%$ of annual cap | **Playbook F: Mandatory Tax Migration** |

## 3. Crisis Action Playbooks

### Playbook A: Household Cash Compression (Trigger: Runway $< 3\text{ Months}$)

**Immediate Objective**: Halt discretionary cash outflows and restore liquid reserve to 6 months.

1. **Immediate Freezes**:
   * Temporarily freeze all non-essential discretionary categories (dining out, streaming, travel funds, non-urgent home improvements).
   * Pause voluntary long-term illiquid investments until emergency reserves are replenished.
2. **Review Recurring Subscriptions**:
   * Financial Controller AI generates an immediate audit list of all monthly recurring charges.
   * Cancel or pause all non-critical subscriptions.
3. **Cash Inflow Acceleration**:
   * Request early dividend distribution or pro-labore from Fix Serviços only if business liquidity is in the Green Zone.
   * Evaluate liquidation of non-essential surplus personal physical assets.

---

### Playbook B: Business Cash Preservation (Trigger: Fix Runway $< 1.5\text{ Months}$)

**Immediate Objective**: Protect payroll/pro-labore, service delivery, and maintain solvency.

1. **Working Capital Quarantine**:
   * Temporarily suspend dividend distributions to household; limit withdrawals strictly to minimum survival pro-labore.
   * Prioritize essential operational payables in order: (1) Mandatory taxes/DAS, (2) Direct job supplies, (3) Critical tools, (4) General overhead.
2. **Accelerated Cash Collections**:
   * Offer a 5% to 8% discount for clients with open invoices who settle within 24 hours.
   * Shift all new service proposals to require a minimum 50% upfront deposit before beginning work.

---

### Playbook C: Client Concentration Defense (Trigger: 1 Client $> 40\%$ of Revenue)

**Immediate Objective**: Mitigate vulnerability to the loss or bankruptcy of a key client.

1. **Commercial Diversification**:
   * The Growth & Pricing AI shifts weekly focus to active pipeline generation for secondary clients.
   * Allocate 4 hours per week specifically to prospecting and reviving past satisfied clients.
2. **Contract Hardening**:
   * Ensure contracts with the primary client have strict 30-to-60 day early termination notice clauses and scheduled milestone payments rather than 100% post-delivery payment.
   * Shorten billing cycles from 30 days to 14 days or bi-weekly.

---

### Playbook D: Receivables Recovery (Trigger: Delinquent Receivables $> 15\%$)

**Immediate Objective**: Recover trapped cash and prevent bad debt write-offs.

1. **Immediate Service Suspension**:
   * Automatically flag delinquent clients in the system; suspend new work until past balances are cleared.
2. **Structured Escalation Protocol**:
   * **Day 1 overdue**: Friendly WhatsApp/Email reminder with PIX copy-paste code.
   * **Day 5 overdue**: Direct telephone call from account manager; check for invoice discrepancy.
   * **Day 15 overdue**: Formal notification proposing an installment workout plan with statutory interest.
   * **Day 30 overdue**: Forward to legal counsel or credit registry (e.g., Protesto de Título / Serasa).

---

### Playbook E: Venture Circuit Breaker (Facilita Vistorias Spend Cap)

**Immediate Objective**: Prevent a startup or speculative enterprise from draining household and core business cash.

1. **The Circuit Breaker Rule**:
   * *Facilita Vistorias* is allocated a hard lifetime testing budget (e.g., R\$ 10.000) for validation.
   * If the budget is exhausted without reaching the predefined unit economics target (e.g., minimum 5 paying clients per week with positive gross margin), the project enters **Automatic Hibernation**.
2. **No Unilateral Cash Injection**:
   * Additional capital injections from household or Fix Serviços are strictly blocked without a formal unanimous joint review and updated business plan approved by both Osmar and Vera.

---

### Playbook F: Mandatory Tax Migration (Trigger: MEI Revenue $> 95\%$ of Cap)

**Immediate Objective**: Avoid tax fines and smoothly transition from MEI to Microempresa (ME) under Simples Nacional.

1. **Immediate Accounting Consultation**:
   * Generate an automated audit package of YTD issued invoices and bank deposits.
   * Schedule transition review with certified accountant.
2. **Price & Margin Recalibration**:
   * Growth & Pricing AI calculates the margin impact of Simples Nacional tax rates (e.g., Annex III or IV, starting at 6% or 4.5% vs. fixed DAS of ~R\$ 75).
   * Adjust quote pricing on new contracts to incorporate future tax rates before the legal transition date.
