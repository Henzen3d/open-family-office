# Templates & Schemas — OpenFamilyOffice

> **Standardized Input Schemas and Executive Reporting Templates.**

---

This directory contains reference schemas and mock data templates for initializing OpenFamilyOffice:

1. [`transactions_template.csv`](./transactions_template.csv): Standard CSV import schema for bank and card transactions, supporting multi-entity tagging and transfer pairing.
2. [`entities_template.csv`](./entities_template.csv): Master registry schema for household and corporate entities (MEI, Simples, LLC, etc.).
3. [`categories_template.csv`](./categories_template.csv): Hierarchical chart of accounts distinguishing household living expenses, business operating costs, cost of goods/services, and tax reserves.
4. [`weekly_briefing_template.md`](./weekly_briefing_template.md): The official 1-page executive briefing template produced by the Family CFO AI for weekly review meetings.
